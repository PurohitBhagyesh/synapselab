1. WHAT ARE THE CONNECTING "THREADS"?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In a neural network, the "threads" (or lines) connecting neurons from one layer
to the next represent SYNAPSES or WEIGHTS ($W$). 
- Each thread carries an optical/numerical signal from a neuron in layer L to a
  neuron in layer L+1.
- In 3D networks, these threads fan out organically as curved 3D Bezier splines
  (like fiber-optic axons or silk threads), carrying animated signal wave pulses.
