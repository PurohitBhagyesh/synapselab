4 LESSONS
LESSON 01 • ANATOMY

What is a Neural Network?

An artificial neural network organizes compute into distinct layers. Data enters through the Input Layer, transforms through intermediate Hidden Layers, and arrives at the Output Layer.

LESSON 02 • SYNAPSES

The Threads (Synapses & Weights)

The luminous white curves connecting dots are synapses. Each thread carries a mathematical weight w that amplifies or dampens data flowing between neurons.

LESSON 03 • MATH & RELU

Inside a Neuron (Activation)

Each neuron computes a linear combination z = ∑(w × x) + b and passes it through an activation function like ReLU: f(z) = max(0, z) to model complex non-linear patterns.

LESSON 04 • DEEP LEARNING

Deep Abstraction & Loss

Adding hidden layers enables hierarchical feature extraction. During training, backpropagation compares network outputs to target labels and minimizes error through gradient descent.

Privacy, Terms & Compliance

Privacy Policy

SynapseLab runs 100% locally in your web browser. No student code, personal data, or architecture designs are ever transmitted to any remote server or third party.

Cookies & Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act Compliance

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or analytics trackers. The only stored data is your local preference for Dark or Light theme saved in standard browser localStorage.

Terms of Use
